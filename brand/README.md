# Brand mark (Draft)

The OpenDAP mark: a lying rhomboid (parallelogram) outline with horizontal top and bottom edges and sides rising diagonally to the right, its two acute corners cut and every corner rounded, drawn as a thick stroke that grows inward (no fill), with a vertical centre line of the same thickness. The outline reads as the **O** and, with the line, the **D** of OpenDAP.

- `mark.py` generates the mark from its parameters (proportions, cut, rounding, stroke), so it can be tuned without redrawing.
- `opendap-mark-a.svg`, `opendap-mark-b.svg`: two first drafts (B: steeper sides, smaller cuts, thicker stroke). `opendap-mark-a-accent.svg`: draft A in the brand orange.
- Status: Draft. Proportions, cut, rounding and stroke are to be confirmed by the maintainers; usage rules (clear space, minimum size, colours) follow once the shape is final.
